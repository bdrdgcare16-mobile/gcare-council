G Care Council (GCC)

G Care Council (GCC) is a premier federation of 425 non-profit organizations committed to skill development, primary healthcare, education, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment across India.

Functioning as an auspicious agency of the Gandhi Foundation, GCC creates a collaborative platform for NGOs, institutions, and development partners to implement structured, scalable, and socially impactful programmes.

The Council is associated with major State and Central Government bodies, supporting training, education, certification, and community-based development initiatives with a strong focus on long-term public benefit.
