Chief Health Ambassador – Course Curriculum

Explore the complete 76-day, day-wise learning roadmap for the Chief Health Ambassador programme — expand any day to view its full set of topics.

COURSE TITLE

Chief Health Ambassador (CHA)

Duration: 3 Months
Eligibility: 10th or 12th Pass
Course Fee: FREE

COURSE CURRICULUM

The Chief Health Ambassador programme is delivered through a structured, day-wise learning plan. Follow the timeline below, jump between curriculum sections, and expand any day to view the complete list of topics covered.

Section 1 · Days 1 – 20

Introduction To Healthcare Delivery Systems, Telehealth Services and Role Of Telehealth Services Coordinator – Introduction, Goals Of Health Care Delivery, Objectives Of Health Care Delivery System, Function Of Health Care Delivery System, Levels Of Health Care Delivery System in India.

Private Sector, Indigenous Systems Of Medicine, Ayurveda and Siddha, Unani and Tibbi, Homeopathy.

Voluntary Health Agencies, Volunteers the Heart Of the Community Functions, National Health Programmes.

Primary Health Care - Concept of Primary Health Care, Principles of Primary Health Care, Elements of Primary Health Care, Primary Level Health Care.

Indian Public Health Standards for PHCs – Objectives, Functions of Primary Health Centre, Local Dias.

Anganwadi Workers – Functions, Accredited Social Health Activist.

Sub Centre Level – Sub Centre, Functions, Indian Public Health Standards for Subcentre – Service Provided: Maternal Care, Antenatal Care, Intranatal Care, Postnatal Care.

Community Health Centre, Indian Public Health Standards for Community Health Centres, Rural Hospitals, District Hospital.

Difference Between Private, Public, And Non – Profit Health Care Delivery System, ESI, Central Government Health Scheme, Facilities.

Difference Between Non – profit Health Care Delivery System to Public Health Care, Difference Between Tee – Medicine, Tele-Education, and Tele Conferencing, Introduction to Tele education & Tele Conferencing, Documentation Required to Practice Telemedicine, also Known as Telehealth, Varies by State.

Licensure, Malpractice, Prescribing.

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPPA) Security, Credentialing, Appointment Schedule System and Billing for Telemedicine.

How to Schedule Patients Effectively – Schedule From Noon, Implement, Prioritize Appointments, Confirm Appointments With Text and Email, Create a Patient Waiting List.

Use Automated Patient Recare and Recall, Queries of Telehealth Services.

Renewal and Identify Proof or Identify Number.

Structure and Functions of Human Body – Introduction, Physiology, Terminologies Used In Anatomy – Based on Terms of Relationship.

Based on Terms of Movements.

Approaches of Anatomy – Regional Anatomy, Systemic Anatomy, Clinical Anatomy, Gross Anatomy, Histological View.

Classification of Structure of Body – General Structure: Primary Tissue, Classification of Primary Tissue in Body, Epithelium, Development of Epithelium Tissue.

Ectodermal Cells, Endodermal Cells, Mesoderm, Classification of Epithilium, Functions of Epithilium, Simple Squamous Epithilium, Funtions, Cuboidal and Columnar Epithelia.

Section 2 · Days 21 – 40

Site of Occurrence, Pseudostratified Epitelium, Sites, Classification, Keratinized Epithelium, Non–Keratinized Epithelium, Stratified Cuboidal.

Stratified Columnar Epithelium, Transitional Epithelium or Urothelium, Glands in the Human Body, Local Hormones and General Hormones-Examples of Local Hormones Are, General Hormones, Major Endocrine Glands, Other Endocrine Glands, Functions of Endocrine Glands.

Different Endocrine Glands and their Secretions, Hypophysis Cerebri or Pituitary, Classification, Adenohypophysis or Anterior Pituitary, Hormones Released, Growth.

Brain and Nervous System, Cardiovascular System, Metabolism of Proteins, Carbohydrates & Lipids, Vitamin Metabolism, Basic Functions of Vital Organs, Structure of Liver, Position, Shapes, Lobes.

Functional or Physiological Lobe, Blood & Nerve Supply Liver - Hepatic Artery, Portal Vein, Functions of Liver, Secretion of Bile, Applied Anatomy - Hepatitis, Hepatic Injury, Liver Biopsy.

Hepatomegaly, Liver Cirrhosis, Liver Transplantation, Physiology of Liver, Composition of Bile, Organic Constituents, Inorganic Constituents.

Difference Between Hepatic (Liver) Bile & Gall Bladder Bile, Bile Salts, Function Of bile, Digestion & Absorption of Fat, Abroption of Fat Soluble Vitamins-A,D,E,K. Regulations of Bile Secretions.

Infection Control Policies & Procedures-Introduction, Infection, Causative Organisms for Infection.

Stages of Infection, Incubation Stage, Prodromal Stage, Illness Stage, Convalescent Stage, Chains Of Infection, organisms, Reservoir, Portal of Exit, Transmission, Portal of Entry, Vulnerable Host.

Concept of Healthy Living, Importance of Infection Control & Prevention, Strategies for Preventing Transmission of Pathogenic Organisms- Hand Hygiene, Environment Hygiene, Screening & Cohorting the Patient.

Vaccination, Surveillance, Appreciating all the Department Which Supports the Infection Prevention Programme, Nosocomial Infection - Definition, Importance of Incident Reporting, Benefits of Incident Reporting.

Concept of Immunization, Difference Between Vaccination & Immunization - Vaccination, Benefits of Immunization, Hand Hygiene, Steps of Hand Wash, & 7 Steps for Handwashing, Duration of hand wash.

After Care Of Handwashing, Hand Rub, Steps for Hand Hygiene, Duration of Hand Rub: 20-30Sec (According to WHO).

Importance of Using Personal Protective Equipment, Personal Protective Equipment, Types of Personal Protective Equipment's, Guidelines for Wearing PPE - Mask, Gloves, Gown.

Procedure Used in Health Care Settings: Five Main Process in Health Care Settings, Vaccination against common infectious Disease.

Apply Good Basic Infection Control Practices, Personal Hygiene, Indications for Hand Hygiene, Reasons for Poor Hand Hygienic Practices Among Health Workers, Hand Hygiene Activities.

World Health Organization Recommendation of Hand Hygiene Procedure, Procedure for Hand Rub.

Safety-Universal or Standard Precautions & Body Mechanics, Basic Life Support, Simplified Basic life Support for Adult.

Jaw Thrust, Adult Cardiac Arrest, BLS Components for Adults, Children & Infants.

Medication Awareness, Medication Administration.

Section 3 · Days 41 – 60

Abbreviation & Description - Drug Administration: Oral Drug Administration.

Parenteral Drug Administrations, Subcutaneous Injections, Intramuscular Injections, Intravascular Injection.

Intravenous Medications: Continuous Drip, Rectal Drug Administration, Conclusion.

Vital Signs (Cardinal Signs) are the TPR & BP, Temperature, Normal & Abnormal Temperature, Types Of Fever.

The Clinical Thermometer, Care of Thermometers, Sites for Taking Temperature.

Temperature Taking & Recording, To Take the Temperature by Rectum.

Pulse-Sites where Pulse Can be Felt, Observations on the Pulse, Taking & Recording the Pulse.

Respiration - Respiration Rate, Counting Respiration's, Abnormalities in Respiration.

Blood Pressure, Variations in Blood Pressure, The BP Apparatus, Method of Taking the Blood Pressure.

Weight & Height - Variations in Weight, Weighing the Patient, Taking Height.

History Taking, Method of Taking History, Recording.

Collection of Blood - Introduction, Arterial Blood Collection, Capillary Blood Collection.

Venous Blood Collection (Venous Puncture), Needle, Syringe, Tourniquet.

Disinfectant, Water Bath or Water Tray, Collection Bottle/Tubes, Procedure.

Specimen Labeling, Use Of Evacuated Tube for Vein Punctures (Vacuum Blood Collection System), Needle, Holder, Vacuum Collection Tubes, Procedure.

Possibilities of Failure in Blood Collection, Rejection of Sample.

Anticoagulants – Introduction, Chemical Anticoagulants, Biological Anticoagulant Heparine.

GDM & GTT, Special Instructions, Fasting Glucose, Insulin, C peptide.

Post Prandial Glucose, Insulin, C peptide, Instruction for Random Glucose, Instruction of Post Glucose.

Instruction for Glucose Challenge Test, Instruction for Lipid Profile Test, Thyroid Test.

Section 4 · Days 61 – 76

Instruction for Stool Analysis, Instruction for Stool Culture.

Urine Routine Examination, Urine Culture, Instruction Slip for Sputum/Culture/Gram Stain/AFB.

Special Instructions, Swabs for Culture, Mantoux Instruction Slips.

Instruction for 24 Hours Urine Analysis, VMA/Metanephrines.

Lipoprotein A, Urine Microalbumin, Drug Level.

Semen Analysis, Master Health Checkup.

Biomedical Waste Management – Biomedical Waste, Biomedical Waste Management, Purpose of Biomedical Waste Management, Importance of Biomedical Waste Management.

Category of Biomedical Waste Management, Medical Waste in the USA, General Waste, Infectious Waste, Hazardous Waste.

Sharp Objects, Surgicals Used For Invasive Procedures, Surgical Instruments, Chemotherapy Drugs, Healthcare Waste in the UK.

World Health Organization (WHO) Medical Waste Classifications.

Types of Colour Coded Bin, Black Colour Bin.

According to Directorate of General of Health Service & Ministry of Health & Family Welfare & Central Pollution Control Board.

Transportation of Biomedical Waste Management, Criteria for Way of Transportation.

Treatment Protocol for Disposal of Biomedical Waste – Yellow Category Waste, Red Category of Waste.

White Category – Segregation, Type of Bag & Container, Treatment & Disposal, Method of Disinfection.

Blue Category, Important Guidelines of Biomedical Waste Management.
