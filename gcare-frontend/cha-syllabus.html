CHA Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Chief Health Ambassador (CHA)

COURSE TITLE

Chief Health Ambassador (CHA)

Duration: 3 Months
Eligibility: 10th or 12th Pass
Course Fee: ₹27,500

SYLLABUS — TOPIC OUTLINE

Topic 1

Public Health and Healthcare Delivery System

  • Levels of healthcare: primary, secondary and tertiary
  • Public health: concept, goals and principles
  • Primary healthcare and its components
  • Village-level and community healthcare services
  • Anganwadi centres, sub-centres, primary health centres and health centres
  • Functions of healthcare personnel and health institutions

Topic 2

Telemedicine and Healthcare Services

  • Telehealth and telemedicine
  • Telemedicine practice, prescribing and patient consultation
  • Appointment scheduling and teleconsultation services
  • Privacy, confidentiality and patient consent
  • Patient identification, registration and documentation

Topic 3

Human Anatomy and Physiology

  • Anatomical terminology, body movements and directional terms
  • Cells: structure, functions and classification
  • Tissues: epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous
  • Organs and organ systems
  • Digestive system and related organs
  • Liver, kidneys and excretory functions

Topic 4

Infection Prevention and Control

  • Importance of infection control
  • Methods to prevent and control infection
  • Immunisation and vaccination
  • Hand hygiene and hand-washing procedure
  • Personal protective equipment and standard precautions

Topic 5

First Aid and Basic Life Support

  • First aid principles and emergency response
  • First aid procedures and assessment
  • Basic life support (BLS)
  • Adult cardiac arrest and cardiopulmonary resuscitation
  • BLS components for adults, children and infants

Topic 6

Laboratory Procedures and Specimen Handling

  • Basic laboratory procedures and safety
  • Specimen collection and handling
  • Blood collection and associated equipment
  • Blood collection tubes and tube additives
  • Anticoagulants: types and uses
  • Instructions and precautions for sample collection

Topic 7

Biomedical Waste Management

  • Introduction to biomedical waste
  • Biomedical waste categories
  • Segregation and colour-coded waste containers
  • Handling and disposal of biomedical waste
  • Treatment and disposal of biomedical waste
