Diploma in Nursing Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Diploma in Nursing

COURSE TITLE

Diploma in Nursing

Duration: 3 Years
Eligibility: 10+2 with Science (PCB)
Certification: Diploma in Nursing
Placement: 95% Placement Assistance

COURSE PAPERS

Paper I

Advanced Nursing Fundamentals

Unit I
  • Advanced Patient Assessment
  • Critical Care Nursing
  • Emergency Nursing Procedures
  • Advanced Life Support
Unit II
  • Pharmacology in Nursing
  • Pain Management
  • Wound Care Management
  • IV Therapy Administration

Paper II

Medical-Surgical Nursing

Unit I
  • Pre-operative Care
  • Post-operative Care
  • Surgical Wound Management
  • Anesthesia Nursing
Unit II
  • Gastrointestinal Surgery
  • Cardiovascular Surgery
  • Orthopedic Surgery
  • Neurosurgery Nursing

PRACTICAL TRAINING

  • Advanced Clinical Procedures
  • Surgical Nursing Practice
  • ICU/CCU Training
  • Emergency Response Drills
  • Medical Administration

💼 Staff Nurse

💼 Nurse Educator
💼 Community Health Nurse
