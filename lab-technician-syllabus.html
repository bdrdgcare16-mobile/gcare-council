Lab Technician Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Diploma in Medical Lab Technician

COURSE TITLE

Diploma in Medical Lab Technician

Duration: 1 Year
Eligibility: 10+2 with Science
Certification: Lab Technician Certificate
Placement: 95% Placement Assistance

COURSE PAPERS

Paper I

Clinical Laboratory Technology

Unit I
  • Introduction to Laboratory Medicine
  • Laboratory Safety
  • Sample Collection Techniques
  • Quality Control in Lab
Unit II
  • Hematology Basics
  • Blood Banking Fundamentals
  • Clinical Chemistry
  • Microbiology Techniques

Paper II

Advanced Laboratory Procedures

Unit I
  • Advanced Hematology
  • Immunology and Serology
  • Histopathology Basics
  • Molecular Diagnostics
Unit II
  • Laboratory Management
  • Automation in Lab
  • Research Methodology
  • Professional Ethics

PRACTICAL TRAINING

  • Blood Sample Collection
  • Urine Analysis Procedures
  • Microscopy Practice
  • Biochemistry Tests
  • Laboratory Internship
