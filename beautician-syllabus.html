Beautician Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Diploma in Beautician / Beauty & Wellness

COURSE TITLE

Diploma in Beautician / Beauty & Wellness

Duration: 1 Year
Eligibility: 10th / 12th Pass
Certification: Diploma in Beautician / Beauty & Wellness
Placement: 95% Placement Assistance

COURSE / SUBJECT SECTIONS

Basic Beauty Therapy

Subjects:

  • Introduction to Beauty Therapy
  • Professional Ethics & Grooming
  • Skin Anatomy and Skin Types
  • Personal Hygiene and Salon Safety
  • Communication Skills

Practicals:

  • Salon Setup Practice
  • Client Consultation
  • Hygiene Procedures

Skin Care & Facial Treatments

Subjects:

  • Basic Facials
  • Advanced Facial Techniques
  • Bleach and Cleanup
  • Acne and Pigmentation Care
  • Facial Machines Basics

Practicals:

  • Fruit Facial
  • Gold Facial
  • Cleanup Procedure
  • Face Massage Techniques

Hair Care & Hair Styling

Subjects:

  • Hair Structure and Hair Types
  • Hair Spa Treatment
  • Hair Cutting Techniques
  • Hair Coloring Basics
  • Hair Straightening & Curling

Practicals:

  • Layer Cut
  • U Cut
  • Hair Spa
  • Blow Dry
  • Hair Styling

Makeup Techniques

Subjects:

  • Basic Makeup
  • Bridal Makeup
  • Party Makeup
  • HD Makeup Introduction
  • Cosmetic Product Knowledge

Practicals:

  • Bridal Makeup Practice
  • Saree Draping
  • Eye Makeup
  • Contouring and Highlighting

Nail & Hand Care

Subjects:

  • Manicure Techniques
  • Pedicure Techniques
  • Nail Art Basics
  • Nail Extension Introduction

Practicals:

  • Spa Manicure
  • Spa Pedicure
  • Basic Nail Art

Spa & Wellness

Subjects:

  • Body Massage Basics
  • Aroma Therapy
  • Spa Treatments
  • Wellness Therapy

Practicals:

  • Body Massage
  • Spa Therapy Session
  • Relaxation Techniques

Additional Subjects

Entrepreneurship Development
