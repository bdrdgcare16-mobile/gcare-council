Mining Sector Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Mining Sector Skill Development

COURSE TITLE

Mining Sector Skill Development

Duration: 3 to 6 Months
Eligibility: Basic Education
Certification: Skill Development Sector Certificate
Placement: 95% Placement Assistance

COURSE PAPERS

Mine Safety Basics

  • Introduction to Mining Operations
  • Mining Safety Regulations
  • Personal Protective Equipment
  • Risk Assessment
Unit II
  • Emergency Procedures
  • Hazard Identification
  • Safety Communication
  • Incident Reporting
