Privacy Policy

G CARE COUNCIL

Website: https://gcarecouncil.in

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

G Care Council respects the privacy of students, educational institutions, applicants, and visitors using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect personal information submitted through our website, including Student Admission Forms, Affiliation Applications, and other educational and training-related services.

By submitting information through our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit an application, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Date of birth and age
  • Gender
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Residential address
  • Parent or guardian information

Educational Information

  • Educational qualifications
  • Course and programme details
  • Admission information
  • Institution details
  • Academic certificates and supporting documents

Uploaded Documents

  • Passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar and other identification documents
  • Educational certificates
  • Parent or guardian signatures
  • Institution affiliation documents
  • Other documents required for applications

We collect information necessary to process the relevant application and provide our educational and administrative services.

3. How We Use Your Information

G Care Council uses collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing student admission applications.
  • Reviewing institutional affiliation applications.
  • Verifying submitted information and documents.
  • Managing educational and training programmes.
  • Communicating application updates.
  • Maintaining administrative records.
  • Providing support to applicants.
  • Improving website functionality and services.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis or required consent.

4. Data Storage and Google Drive Integration

G Care Council uses database and file-storage systems to manage information submitted through its website.

Structured application information may be stored in a MySQL database.

Uploaded documents may be stored using authorized file-storage services, including Google Drive, where that integration is configured and operational.

Google Drive integration is intended to support the storage, retrieval, and management of documents submitted through G Care Council applications.

Google Drive authorization is used for application-related file operations, not for unrelated access to a user's personal files.

The availability of an uploaded document depends on successful completion of its storage process.

5. Google API Services and User Data

Where G Care Council uses Google API Services, access to Google account data is limited to the permissions required for the relevant application functionality.

Information obtained through Google APIs will be used only for the purposes disclosed to users and permitted by the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy.

We do not use Google API data for unrelated advertising, sell it to data brokers, or use it to develop unrelated artificial intelligence models.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Information Sharing

We may share information with authorized personnel and service providers when necessary to:

  • Process admission and affiliation applications.
  • Verify submitted documents.
  • Deliver educational services.
  • Provide application-related communication.
  • Maintain our website and storage infrastructure.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell applicants' personal information.

7. Data Security

G Care Council is committed to protecting personal information and uploaded documents from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

We seek to maintain appropriate technical and organizational safeguards for our website, database, document storage, and administrative access.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information and submitted documents for as long as reasonably necessary to process applications, maintain required administrative records, provide services, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise manage it in accordance with applicable requirements.

Applicants may contact G Care Council to request information about their records or ask for correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Children's and Students' Privacy

Where an applicant is a minor, information should be submitted by or with the involvement and authorization of a parent or lawful guardian, as required.

G Care Council recognizes the importance of protecting students' personal information and educational records.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, individuals may contact G Care Council to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent and withdrawal is permitted.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

11. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, database management, document storage, email communication, and SMS notifications.

These service providers may process information only as necessary for their respective services and applicable obligations.

Relevant third-party services may include Google Drive and the website's hosting and communication providers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

G Care Council may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in website functionality, data processing practices, service providers, or applicable requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact Us
