Electrical Engineering Syllabus

Complete course curriculum for Diploma in Electrical Engineering

COURSE TITLE

Diploma in Electrical Engineering

Duration: 1 Year
Eligibility: A Pass in 10th Standard
Certification: Electrical Engineering Diploma
Placement: 95% Placement Assistance

COURSE PAPERS

Paper I

Basic Electrical Engineering

Unit I
  • Introduction to Electrical Engineering
  • Basic Circuit Theory
  • Ohm's Law and Kirchhoff's Laws
  • Electrical Measurements
Unit II
  • AC and DC Circuits
  • Transformers
  • Electrical Machines Basics
  • Power Generation

Paper II

Electrical Machines and Control

Unit I
  • DC Machines
  • AC Machines
  • Motor Control
  • Generator Operations
Unit II
  • Power Systems
  • Transmission Lines
  • Distribution Systems
  • Electrical Safety

PRACTICAL TRAINING

  • Electrical Wiring Practice
  • Machine Lab Practical
  • Power System Laboratory
  • Control System Lab
  • Industrial Training
