Chairman Message

A message of vision, purpose & commitment

“

I am pleased to announce the continued expansion and strengthening of G Care Council (GCC) as a leading platform for vocational education, skill development, and community-based capacity building, functioning under the auspicious guidance of the Gandhi Foundation.It is my privilege to extend a wide range of Vocational Education and Training programs across diverse sectors such as Healthcare, Engineering Trades, Electrical, Civil, Fire & Safety, and emerging skill domains, designed to meet the evolving demands of industry and society.

Education is the foundation of professional growth and social transformation. A skilled and educated individual not only builds a sustainable livelihood but also contributes meaningfully to society by creating opportunities and delivering value to the community. Across the world, nations are increasingly investing in skill-based education systems, recognizing that the future workforce must be equipped with specialized, practical, and industry-relevant competencies.In India, while significant progress has been made in vocational education, the benefits have yet to fully reach the grassroots level, particularly among those who possess skills but lack formal certification.

G Care Council (GCC) is c​ommitted to bridging this critical gap by identifying such individuals and providing them with recognized certification, structured training, and pathways to employment and entrepreneurship.Drawing from the importance of career-oriented education and employability, GCC has developed comprehensive and industry-aligned curricula, integrating:· Practical skill training · Core technical knowledge · Employability and life skills · Internship and apprenticeship opportunities We firmly believe that a certificate alone does not define a career—it is the combination of knowledge, skill, and application that shapes a successful professional journey.

Our programs are carefully designed to ensure that learners become industry-ready professionals, capable of securing employment in private and public sectors, as well as pursuing self-employment opportunities.With a strong commitment to quality, relevance, and accessibility, G Care Council aims to create a new generation of skilled, certified, and competent practitioners, contributing to the nation’s development and economic growth.I am confident that through our collective efforts, we will continue to empower individuals, strengthen communities, and build a skilled and self-reliant India.

Chairman, G Care Council
